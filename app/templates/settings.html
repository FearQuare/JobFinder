LinkedIn account

Public LinkedIn job search works without an account. Connecting your account loads search results the way you see them when signed in, with more results per search.

A browser window opens and you sign in there. The app never sees or stores your password. It keeps that browser session in the app's data folder and reuses it when collecting.

LinkedIn's User Agreement forbids automated access, and accounts that trip its detection can be restricted. The app goes slowly to make this unlikely, but the risk is on your account. Public mode doesn't use your account at all.
